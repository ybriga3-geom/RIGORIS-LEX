Your objections

The questions people ask us before starting

Are you a law firm?

No. RIGORIS LEX is an advisory company: we work within the limits of activities not reserved for regulated professions. We represent clients with authorities and institutions; court representation is handled by attorneys, with whom we coordinate.

Why one contact rather than several specialists?

Because projects often fail between two specialists. We coordinate study, structuring, formation and follow-up, and bring in partner experts when needed.

Isn't AI or an online search enough?

They give leads, not a file: repealed texts, missing Moroccan context, no follow-up. Our platforms use technology, but every recommendation is checked and owned by a consultant.

How much will it cost?

The first call is free. After that, each engagement has a fixed fee or quote agreed before any commitment: no surprise invoices.

You are a young company: why trust you?

Because everything we state can be verified: every recommendation cites its references, and every step is documented in writing.

A project to study, structure or manage?

Tell us about your situation: we will reply with an initial direction, with no obligation.
