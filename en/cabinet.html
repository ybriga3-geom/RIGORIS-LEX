A Moroccan company serving investors

Based in Marrakech, RIGORIS LEX brings together investor advisory, project structuring, digital, publishing and training.

Our story

RIGORIS LEX was born from an observation: in Morocco, projects rarely fail for lack of an idea, but often for lack of scoping, structuring and follow-up.

Our name combines two ideas: rigor, exactness, and lex, the rule. It sums up how we work — start from the texts, study the project precisely, and deliver recommendations we can stand behind in writing.

We support investors and project sponsors from idea to operation, and extend this advice through digital platforms, publications and training.

Our scope

RIGORIS LEX carries out its advisory activities within the limits of activities not reserved by law for regulated professions. Court representation is exclusively a matter for attorneys, with whom we coordinate when needed.
